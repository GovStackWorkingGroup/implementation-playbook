# B.1 Define the Vision and Future Experience\*

Define the Future vision\



This 15 minute warm up activity helps teams define a clear future vision for the service that will guide ideation and to-be mapping.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Future vision (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary><strong>How to do it</strong></summary>

In groups, describe what the future service should look and feel like.

Ask groups to answer these questions:

* **What is the future service?**
* **How does it help users?**
* **What are the most important qualities or features?**

They can:

* Write short statements
* Use post-it notes
* Sketch a simple, high-level illustration



</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Outputs</strong></summary>

* [ ] One future vision per group (written or sketched).
* [ ] A clear reference point for ideation and to-be mapping.

</details>
